3.11 Bonding Conductor - A protective conductor
providing equipotential bonding.
3.12 Bonding Network (BN) - A set of
interconnected conductive parts that provide a path for
current at frequencies from direct current (d.c.) to radio
frequency (RF) intended to divert, block or impede the
passage of electromagnetic energy.
3.13 Bonding Ring Conductor (BRC) -A bus
earthing conductor in the form of a closed ring.
NOTE - Normally the bonding ring conductor, as part of the
bonding network, has multiple connections to the common
bonding network (CBN) that improves its performance.
3.14 Booth - Non-stationary unit intended to
accommodate equipment generally for pleasure or
demonstration purpose.
3.15 Building Voids, Accessible - Space within the
structure or the components of a building accessible
only at certain points.
NOTES
1 Examples are: Space within partitions, suspended floors,
ceilings and certain types of window frame, door frame and
architraves.
2 Specially formed building voids are also as ducts.
3.16 Building Void, Non-accessible -A space within
the structure or the components of building which has
no ready means of access.
