411. General

The transformer shall be rated for continuous
operation at 40°C ambient temperature. The
winding configuration shall be Dyn11 as
specified in 411.3.3. The transfor-
mer shall meet the requirements of Table 1.

411.3 Verification

All tests shall be performed at the factory
before shipment. The low-voltage winding
is tested at 2.5 kV for 60 seconds per
IEC 60076-3. The specification requires
N+1 redundancy on all auxiliary circuits.
