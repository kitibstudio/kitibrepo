# Specification Extract: Protection and Isolation

This document is an extract prepared for tender. Clause numbers refer to the governing standard and are quoted so that the citation manager can resolve them.

## Additional protection

411.3.3 Additional protection by a residual current device shall be provided for socket-outlets with a rated current not exceeding 32 A that are intended for general use by ordinary persons.

Where the requirements of Regulation 411.3.3 cannot be met, supplementary equipotential bonding shall be installed in accordance with Regulation 415.2 and recorded on the certificate.

## Isolation

Every circuit shall be capable of being isolated from each live supply conductor. The isolation requirements of clause 7.2 are supplemented by the following project-specific requirements.

- Isolation devices shall be lockable in the open position.
- A durable label shall identify the circuit isolated by each device.
- Isolation of the main LV panel shall not remove the supply to the fire alarm system.

## Rating factors

Table 4-2 gives the grouping factors applied to circuits installed in the same containment. Where a circuit is installed in free air for part of its route, the more onerous factor applies over the whole route.

| Installation method | Circuits grouped | Factor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enclosed in conduit | 2 | 0.80 |
| Enclosed in conduit | 3 | 0.70 |
| Cable tray, touching | 4 | 0.77 |
| Cable tray, spaced | 4 | 0.94 |

## Equipment requirements

The following requirements apply to all switchgear supplied under this specification.

1. Type-tested assemblies only. Partially type-tested assemblies are not acceptable.
2. Short-circuit withstand rated for the prospective fault current at the point of installation, with a minimum duration of one second.
3. Cross-sectional area of protective conductors selected by calculation, not by the adiabatic table, where the disconnection time exceeds 0.4 s.
4. Three-phase equipment shall be labelled with its phase rotation at the point of connection.

## Materials

Cable construction is XLPE/SWA/PVC throughout. Flexible connections at motor terminal boxes are permitted only where shown on the drawings, and shall be self-contained assemblies with factory-fitted glands.

Star-delta starters shall be supplied with a mechanical interlock between the star and delta contactors. The low-voltage control circuit shall be derived from the same supply as the power circuit.
